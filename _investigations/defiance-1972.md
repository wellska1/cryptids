## What is reported

Before the week was over, Defiance had turned one strange sighting into a town-wide mystery watch.

In August 1972, Defiance residents reported a wolf-headed figure near railroad tracks and industrial edges of town. The setting itself helped the story stick: narrow corridors, limited light, and long sightlines where any movement looked dramatic.

Newspaper coverage transformed isolated observations into a full local episode. People compared stories, mapped likely locations, and treated each new night as a chance for another encounter.

Within days, the town had a shared script: who heard what, which stretch of track mattered most, and which night felt like the one when the mystery nearly came into focus.
