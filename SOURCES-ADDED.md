# Historical source additions (Version 4)

Each source is linked in `_data/evidence.yml` and appears on the corresponding investigation and evidence dossier pages.

- **Lake Erie (1892):** Library of Congress digitized *New-York Tribune* July 10 issue and *Democratic Northwest* July 21 issue. Article placement must still be checked against scans. Earlier July 17 reference is not treated as verified.
- **Minerva (1980):** Barbara Mudrak, *Akron Beacon Journal*, June 29, 1980, reproduced in a July 1980 clipping compilation. BFRO Stark County report index added as a separate secondary lead.
- **Defiance (1972):** Ellen Armstrong *Crescent-News* transcription, James Stegall *The Blade* transcription, and Toledo History Box retrospective. The Blade date has been corrected to August 3; Crescent-News dates conflict across sources and remain flagged.

No copyrighted newspaper scans are bundled. All original-document flags remain false until individually authenticated. URLs can change; review periodically.
