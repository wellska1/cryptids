## What the labels mean

**Documented publication:** A specific historical publication can be identified. This does not validate its claims. **Secondary account:** An account reported by another person or later publication. **Reported:** An alleged event or witness interpretation. **Unverified lead:** An original record has not been located or authenticated. **Folklore:** A regional story without an established original incident.

## Editorial principles

1. Link each historical claim to a traceable source wherever possible.
2. Distinguish contemporary reports from later retellings.
3. Preserve uncertainty in dates, names, and geographic coordinates.
4. Never represent illustrations as eyewitness photographs or archival evidence.
5. Do not publish private witness contact information without permission.
6. Check copyright and licensing before hosting newspaper scans or submitted photographs.

## Current archive status

This starter site contains sample case files and research leads. Bibliographic references and links are **starting points for verification**, not a completed authenticated archive. No historical photograph or original newspaper scan is included in this release.

To propose a correction, update the relevant Markdown or YAML source in the project repository and include a citation and a note explaining the change.
